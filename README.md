# RUNTIME — roundups & newsletters

A public archive of RUNTIME's weekly AI roundups, newsletters and follow-up notes.
Each issue will bring together what we covered, why it matters, original sources,
and links to the videos and practical guides.

- [Read the archive](posts/README.md)
- [Watch RUNTIME on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@runtime-weekly)
- [Runnable tutorials and setup guides](https://github.com/Runtime-weekly/runtime-tutorials)

## What belongs here

Published roundups, newsletters, corrections and useful references. Runnable
examples belong in separate tutorial repositories so their setup instructions,
dependencies and releases are easy to find.

Each article states its publication date, links to primary sources and separates
what we tested from what a project or paper reports. Older posts describe what
was available when they were written; corrections are dated in the article.

## Archive format

Issues use `posts/YYYY-MM-DD-short-title.md` and appear in the archive with the
newest first. The [issue template](templates/issue.md) shows the format.
There are no published newsletter issues in this repository yet.

## Corrections

Open an issue with the article link, the passage and a supporting public source.
Please do not include passwords, personal details, private logs or customer data.
Code examples are licensed in their respective tutorial repositories; this
archive does not change the rights in linked papers, models or videos.
